# Rights and source attribution

Review date: 13 November 2026. All supplied source files are unchanged copies;
their original notices and filenames are retained. Publication does not imply
endorsement by the original publishers. The project does not apply its own
software licence to these third-party documents. The document/file manifests
identify the publisher, title, official source, frozen hash and applicable
rights basis for each item.

## Supplied originals

- **EU legal and policy documents (`EU_REUSE`).** The
  [EUR-Lex legal notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/content/legal-notice/legal-notice.html?locale=en)
  permits reuse of legal documents for commercial and non-commercial purposes
  unless a specific exception applies. Sources are credited individually below;
  no specific third-party exception was identified in these frozen legal texts.
- **UK government material (`UK_OGL3`).** The acquired pages and
  [GOV.UK reuse policy](https://www.gov.uk/help/reuse-govuk-content), together with
  [the legislation service's reuse statement](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/contributors),
  identify the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).
  Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence
  v3.0. Source attributions are listed below. The OGL permits distribution and
  requires attribution; third-party rights remain excluded. Integral document
  marks are retained, not adopted as project branding.
- **Scottish Government and Revenue Scotland (`SCOT_OGL3`, `REVSCOT_OGL3`).**
  The frozen HTML includes OGL3 notices. The
  [Scottish Government policy](https://www.gov.scot/crown-copyright/) and
  [Revenue Scotland terms](https://revenue.scot/privacy/legal-general) reserve
  third-party/branding rights. Only the acquired HTML files are included;
  linked logo, thumbnail and graphic asset files are not copied. SVG elements
  in these snapshots reference external icon assets rather than bundling
  separate graphical works.
- **DigCompEdu (`JRC_DIGCOMPEDU`).** The
  [report-specific permission](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/scientific-activities/key-competences-lifelong-learning/digcompedu/digcompedu-translation-and-use_en)
  permits copying and redistribution with acknowledgement and preservation of
  meaning. The frozen PDF identifies all images as EU-owned. Credit: Redecker,
  C. (2017), *European Framework for the Digital Competence of Educators:
  DigCompEdu*, edited by Y. Punie, European Commission/JRC, EUR 28775 EN,
  doi:10.2760/159770. No changes were made.

## Originals retained with official links

These decisions concern redistribution of complete unchanged frozen files,
not whether the text may be read, cited or used in approved research.

- **DOC008, DOC009:** Norwegian ministerial strategy/plan text may fall within
  [section 14 of the Norwegian Copyright Act](https://lovdata.no/nav/lov/2018-06-15-40/kap1).
  Its scope excludes certain reused and artistic material. No explicit
  complete-file reuse grant or confirmation for every embedded figure was
  established; retain the original PDFs and seek confirmation for full-file
  redistribution. This is not a claim that the official text is protected.
- **DOC010, DOC012, DOC013:** the full PDFs contain externally credited photos
  or illustrations. [Government image terms](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/om-nettstedet/right-of-use-and-copyright-for-photos-and-video-material/id2715005/)
  require separate clearance for third-party material. Examples include NTB,
  Colourbox, private photographers and illustrations used by permission.
- **DOC014:** the PEFC notice restricts changes and commercial copying. A clear
  grant for public repository redistribution of the complete file was not
  established; clarify permission for unchanged academic redistribution.
- **DOC015:** the FSC copyright page requires written publisher permission
  for copying; the cover also credits third-party photos.
- **DOC032:** DigComp 3.0 has CC BY 4.0 terms for EU-owned content but expressly
  excludes Adobe Stock decorative and chapter images on its copyright page.
  That text licence does not clear the complete unchanged PDF.
- **DOC033–DOC035:** each Council of Europe volume reserves reproduction and
  transmission rights and requires written permission. The notices also credit
  third-party photography.
- **DOC037:** the UNESCO text is CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO, but its cover credits
  Shutterstock images. [UNESCO's terms](https://www.unesco.org/en/open-access/cc-sa)
  limit the automatic licence to text and require permission for images not
  identified as UNESCO-owned or public domain. The intact PDF is link-only.
- **DOC040:** DigCompOrg permits attributed reproduction but excepts the
  f/sco/Fotolia cover photograph; complete-file redistribution is not cleared.
- **DOC052:** the [Highland Council publication scheme](https://www.highland.gov.uk/information-performance-statistics/guide-information)
  allows attributed reproduction of Council-owned information. The frozen PDF
  includes a Green Freeport executive-summary appendix and graphics whose
  complete redistribution rights were not established. Do not extend that
  general permission to every appended work without confirmation.
- **DOC053:** the [Edinburgh reuse page](https://www.edinburgh.gov.uk/managing-information/re-using-council%E2%80%99s-information)
  directs applicants to obtain a reuse licence. An older scheme allows copying
  Council-owned information, but no clear complete-file grant was established
  for this frozen business-case/referral PDF. Its original council URL and
  recorded official platform redirect are both retained.

No covers, images, notices or pages were removed to make a new file appear to
be the original. Permission decisions can be revised with publisher evidence;
the frozen source bytes, IDs and checksums must remain unchanged.
