# Figure 1 — IEEE caption

**Execution-resource profile across benchmark conditions.**
Panels (a) and (b) show empirical cumulative distributions of total
token consumption and mean end-to-end latency, respectively, using
30 task-level observations per condition. Each task-level value
aggregates the three benchmark repetitions for that task-condition
combination. Panels (c) and (d) show the proportions of tasks requiring
different numbers of model and retrieval calls. B0 is the conventional
two-step RAG reference, while B1 and G1 are the matched agentic
conditions. The figure reports execution-derived resource and runtime
measurements only; no LLM-judge or expert-evaluation outcomes are used.
