# DOC905 - High-Risk Travel Addendum

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-06-01
Status: Synthetic engineering document

## High-risk destinations

Travel to a destination classified as high risk requires approval from both the
employee's department head and the security officer before booking.

## Relationship to general travel policy

This addendum supplements DOC901.

For travel classified as high risk, the approval requirements in this addendum
take precedence over the ordinary international-travel approval requirement in
DOC901.
