# DOC903 - Project Access Control Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-02-01
Status: Synthetic engineering document

## External collaborators

External collaborators require approval from the project owner before access is
granted.

Multi-factor authentication must be enabled before the account becomes active.

## Access review

Access granted to external collaborators must be reviewed at least once every
three months.

## Privileged access

Temporary privileged access requires approval from the system owner in addition
to the normal project access requirements.
