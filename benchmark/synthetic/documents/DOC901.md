# DOC901 - Travel and Expense Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-01
Status: Synthetic engineering document

## International travel

International business travel must be approved by the employee's department
head before travel is booked.

## Expense documentation

Employees must submit receipts for reimbursable travel expenses.

## Scope

This policy applies to ordinary domestic and international business travel.
Additional requirements may apply to travel classified as high risk.
