## Use-case definition

UC2 studies authoritative public policy and implementation material governing
Scottish Green Freeports.

Working title:

**Scottish Green Freeports: policy design, governance, fiscal and customs
mechanisms, planning and consenting, and implementation of Forth and
Inverness and Cromarty Firth Green Freeports.**

## In-scope policy layers

A source is within the UC2 discovery boundary when it directly defines,
governs, operationalises, legally designates, evaluates, or implements:

1. the Scottish Green Freeport policy model;
2. programme governance and business-case requirements;
3. Green Freeport fiscal mechanisms applicable in Scotland;
4. customs mechanisms explicitly applicable to Scottish Green Freeports;
5. planning and consenting arrangements for Scottish Green Freeports;
6. legal designation of Scottish Green Freeport tax sites; or
7. implementation of either:
   - Inverness and Cromarty Firth Green Freeport; or
   - Forth Green Freeport.

## Generic UK Freeport material

Generic UK Freeport material is not automatically in scope.

It may become eligible only when the issuing authority explicitly states that
the material applies to Scottish Green Freeports, or when it defines a
mechanism directly used by the Scottish Green Freeport programme.

## Source-authority boundary

Candidate corpus sources should originate from authoritative public bodies,
including:

- Scottish Government;
- UK Government departments;
- HM Revenue & Customs;
- Revenue Scotland;
- legislation.gov.uk / official legislation;
- accountable or participating Scottish local authorities.

Commercial promotional material, consultancy commentary, press coverage,
academic commentary, advocacy material, and operator marketing are not primary
UC2 corpus sources.

They may be used only as discovery/context material if necessary and must not
be promoted into the corpus without a separately recorded methodological
justification.

## Maps and primarily graphical sources

Maps may be retained as provenance or contextual material.

A map or other primarily graphical artifact is not automatically a RAG corpus
document unless it contains sufficient independently useful textual evidence
and later satisfies the frozen eligibility criteria.

## Candidate-versus-corpus separation

A source appearing in the candidate inventory means only that it has been
identified for later assessment.

Candidate identification does NOT mean:

- accepted corpus membership;
- benchmark-active membership;
- eligibility;
- retrieval suitability; or
- scientific evidence of system performance.

## Chronology guard

The following must occur prospectively:

1. finish source discovery;
2. freeze formal eligibility criteria;
3. acquire and verify candidate artifacts;
4. apply the frozen criteria at document level;
5. establish final corpus membership;
6. perform deterministic preprocessing;
7. materialize embeddings;
8. freeze engineering retrieval-diagnostic protocols;
9. qualify retrieval configuration;
10. freeze the retrieval foundation.

Benchmark-task construction and scientific benchmark execution occur only
after the relevant experimental foundations are frozen.
