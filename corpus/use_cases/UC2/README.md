# UC2 — Scottish Green Freeports

## Current benchmark scope

UC2 is one of the three document contexts in the completed thesis benchmark.
The [frozen scope](benchmark_scope.json) contains 17 accepted,
retrieval-active documents (DOC041–DOC053 and DOC055–DOC058); DOC054 was
excluded by the frozen eligibility criteria before benchmark execution.
The primary benchmark defines tasks T011–T020 in
[benchmark/tasks/UC2](../../../benchmark/tasks/UC2/).

## Provenance and acquisition

The acquisition plan, source inventory, eligibility review and retrieval
foundation records in this directory document the historical selection and
qualification steps. Their pre-execution status statements are dated research
checkpoints, not the current study status. The
[corpus overview](../../README.md) and primary benchmark freeze distinguish
later completion from those earlier records.

Original files and derived retrieval artifacts were frozen for the benchmark.
DOC051 is one logical document acquired in nine HTML components; its component
filenames and order must not be silently replaced or flattened.

## Public source release

The [Study A source-document package](../../../research-artifacts/study-a/source-documents/README.md)
contains the 15 UC2 documents whose reviewed rights support redistribution,
with original file bytes, source credits and checksums. DOC052 and DOC053 are
link-only pending redistribution clarification. The released source subset is
not the complete frozen UC2 retrieval corpus, and does not on its own enable
full retrieval replay.
