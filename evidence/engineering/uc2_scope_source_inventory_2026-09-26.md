# UC2 Scope and Initial Official-Source Inventory

Date: 2026-09-26
Phase: UC2-P0/P1
Mode: SOURCE_DISCOVERY_ONLY

## Purpose

Establish the working UC2 boundary and record an initial inventory of
authoritative public candidate sources before document acquisition,
eligibility adjudication, corpus construction, retrieval evaluation, or
benchmark execution.

## Use case

UC2 — Scottish Green Freeports.

The working boundary covers policy design, governance, fiscal and customs
mechanisms, planning and consenting, legal tax-site designation, and
site-specific implementation for:

- Inverness and Cromarty Firth Green Freeport; and
- Forth Green Freeport.

## Discovery authority classes

Initial discovery was restricted to authoritative public sources from:

- Scottish Government;
- UK Government;
- HM Revenue & Customs;
- Revenue Scotland;
- legislation.gov.uk;
- The Highland Council; and
- The City of Edinburgh Council.

## Initial candidate inventory

An initial set of thirteen candidate sources was identified.

A subsequent pre-eligibility completeness audit added five authoritative
fiscal/support sources (UC2-C14 through UC2-C18), bringing the working
candidate inventory to eighteen.

The inventory is a discovery record only.

No candidate has been adjudicated or accepted into the UC2 corpus.

Two local-government business-case candidates (UC2-C12 and UC2-C13) are
currently represented by authoritative landing/committee/disclosure pages.
Their exact downloadable artifacts must be resolved during the later
acquisition phase before any eligibility decision.

## Methodological guard

Candidate identification is not eligibility.

The formal UC2 eligibility rubric has not yet been written or frozen.

No source in this inventory may be described as an accepted corpus document
until the frozen eligibility criteria have been applied at document level.

Generic UK Freeport material is not automatically eligible; explicit
applicability to Scottish Green Freeports or direct operational relevance is
required under the working scope contract.
