# Paper / Thesis Scope Separation v0.1

Date: 2026-09-27

Status: prospective, pre-primary-benchmark.

## Thesis scope

The MSc thesis retains the complete three-use-case design:

- UC1: T001-T010
- UC2: T011-T020
- UC3: T021-T030

All three conditions (B0, B1, G1) and all three repetitions remain in scope.

The full primary technical benchmark therefore remains:

30 tasks × 3 conditions × 3 repetitions = 270 runs.

The thesis may integrate technical results, human evaluation/interviews,
and cross-use-case analysis across UC1, UC2 and UC3.

## Paper scope

The planned paper is intentionally narrower and uses UC1 only.

Technical paper scope:

- UC1 only
- T001-T010
- B0, B1 and G1
- three repetitions

Therefore the technical paper subset contains:

10 tasks × 3 conditions × 3 repetitions = 90 runs.

The paper's qualitative component is also restricted to the interviews,
interview material, coding and analysis attributable to UC1.

If a participant discusses more than one use case, only UC1-attributable
segments are eligible for the paper analysis. The complete interview material
may remain available for the thesis analysis according to the approved
research protocol.

## Scientific relationship

The paper is a focused subset of the broader thesis study.

The primary technical comparison remains B1 versus G1.
B0 versus B1 versus G1 remains supporting context.

No task, configuration, workflow, retrieval setup, model setting,
execution order, repetition count or primary benchmark implementation is
changed by this publication-scope decision.

The complete 270-run benchmark will still be executed in the already frozen
order. The paper will later select the predetermined UC1 subset from those
results.

This scope decision was recorded before any T001-T030 primary benchmark run
was executed and before any primary benchmark result was inspected.
